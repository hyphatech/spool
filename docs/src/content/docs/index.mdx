<Features>
  <Feature title="In your transaction" icon="shield-check" href="/spool/guide/enqueueing/" linkText="Enqueueing">
    A job is a row written with the work that made it owed, so a rollback
    leaves no job.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Several processes, one queue" icon="layers" href="/spool/guide/runner/" linkText="The runner">
    Claims that never wait on each other, leases a heartbeat renews, and a
    lapsed one rescued.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Retries with backoff" icon="wrench" href="/spool/guide/handlers/" linkText="Handlers">
    A handler retries, retries after what an upstream said, or discards.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Asked twice, one job" icon="boxes" href="/spool/guide/enqueueing/#once-for-a-key" linkText="Unique keys">
    A unique key folds a second request into the job already waiting.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Scheduled and prioritised" icon="list-checks" href="/spool/guide/enqueueing/#when-it-runs" linkText="When it runs">
    At an instant, after a span, until an expiry, at a priority from 0 to 9.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Tests that never sleep" icon="zap" href="/spool/guide/testing/" linkText="Testing">
    `Runner.drain` runs every job due at the test's own clock.
  </Feature>
</Features>
